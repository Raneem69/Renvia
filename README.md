# 🧠 MIND

### Your second brain. Your personal AI. Your world.

MIND is a personalized AI companion designed to become part of your everyday life.

Instead of simply answering questions, MIND learns your preferences, remembers what matters to you, helps organize your day, and gives you access to powerful AI tools through one smooth and customizable experience.

## ✨ Features

- 💬 AI conversations
- 🧠 Personalized long-term memory
- 📅 Tasks, reminders & daily planning
- 🌤️ Weather & location-based assistance
- 🎤 Voice messages & real-time AI voice conversations
- 🌍 Multiple languages & accents
- 🎨 Custom themes, aesthetics & app icons
- ✨ AI image & video generation
- 🔎 Web Search & Deep Search
- 📄 Document & image analysis
- 🎵 Spotify & Apple Music integration
- 👤 AI personality customization
- 🔐 Privacy & personalization controls
- 💎 Free & premium experiences

## 🎯 Vision

MIND is built around one simple idea:

> **Don't make the user organize their life for the AI. Let the AI organize it for them.**

MIND aims to become a personal AI ecosystem that gets more useful the longer you use it.
